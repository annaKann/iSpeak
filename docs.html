Step 01

Creating Your Account

New to iSpeak? Sign up in seconds. Already have an account? Jump straight to login.

Sign Up

Login

  • 1

    Open iSpeak

    You'll land on the Login / Sign Up screen. Tap the Signup tab at the top right if you're new.

  • 2

    Enter Your Details

    Fill in your Name, Email, Password, and Confirm Password in the fields provided.

  • 3

    Tap Create Account

    Hit the blue Create Account button. You'll be taken straight into the app to set up your profile.

  • 4

    Returning User?

    Select the Login tab, enter your email and password, and you're in.

Step 02

Setting Up Your Profile

This is the foundation of your iSpeak experience. Your goal and reward are what drive your personalized motivation.

Profile Setup Screen

  • 1

    Enter Your Display Name

    Type the name you'd like iSpeak to greet you with on the home screen.

  • 2

    Set Your Personal Goal

    Be specific — "Run a 5K in 30 days" or "Get in shape for beach trip." This powers your AI motivation messages.

  • 3

    Choose a Target Date

    Pick the date you want to achieve your goal by. This creates urgency and keeps you on track.

  • 4

    Add Your Reward

    What do you get when you crush it? A new outfit, a trip, dinner out — your reward reminds you why it's worth it.

  • 5

    Tap Start Your Experience

    Hit the button and you're in. Your home screen is now personalized to you.

Step 03

Navigating the Home Screen

Everything you need for a workout is right here — no digging around required.

Home Screen

  • 1

    Your Greeting

    At the top you'll see "Hi, [Your Name]! Ready To Get Started?" — a personalized welcome every time you open the app.

  • 2

    Your Active Goal Card

    The card below your greeting shows your current goal and target date. Tap it to receive an AI-generated motivational message tied to your goal.

  • 3

    Choose Your Voice

    Tap the + button to browse and select your motivator — athletes, celebrities, or coaches.

  • 4

    Select Intensity Mode

    Pick from Light, Standard, or Intense to match your energy level for the day.

  • 5

    Start Workout

    Hit the blue Start Workout button when you're ready to go. The bottom nav bar gives you access to Home, Progress, and Profile from anywhere.

Step 04

Choosing Your Voice

Your motivator is the heart of iSpeak. Browse by category, filter by type, or search for someone specific.

Browse Voices

Search

  • 1

    Tap the + Button

    From the home screen, tap the large + under "Choose Your Voice" to open the voice selection screen.

  • 2

    Quick Options

    Not sure who to pick? Tap Hype Trainer, Calm Coach, or Drill Sergeant for an instant preset style.

  • 3

    Filter by Category

    Use the All / Athletes / Celebrities / Coaches tabs to browse voices by type.

  • 4

    Search for Someone

    Use the search bar at the top to find a specific person by name — like typing "Jessica" to find Jessica Alba, Jessica Chastain, and more.

  • 5

    Select & Confirm

    Tap the play button next to any voice to preview it, then select the one that fires you up most.

The Rock Oprah Ariana Grande LeBron James Mike Tyson + More
Step 05

Your Motivation Card

Before you start your workout, tap your goal card on the home screen to get a personalized AI push tied to your exact goal and reward.

Goal Card

  • 1

    Tap Your Goal Card

    From the home screen, tap the goal card showing your active goal and target date.

  • 2

    See Your Goal & Reward

    The card shows your personal goal — for example "Get in shape for beach" — and your chosen reward like "New Swim Swimsuit."

  • 3

    Read Your AI Message

    At the bottom of the card, iSpeak generates a unique motivational message that connects your goal, your reward, and your timeline — like "You're only 30 days away from feeling amazing on that beach. Every workout counts. Let's crush it today."

Tip: Tap this card before every session to get that mental boost before you even press start.
Step 06

Starting Your Workout

Your voice is selected, your intensity is set — now it's time to move.

Live Workout Session

  • 1

    Tap Start Workout

    From the home screen, confirm your voice and intensity then tap the Start Workout button.

  • 2

    See Your Motivator

    Your selected voice's photo appears on screen inside a glowing circle, so you know exactly who's in your corner pushing you through.

  • 3

    Watch the Timer

    The live timer tracks your session duration. Push yourself — every minute counts toward your streak.

  • 4

    Pause or Stop Anytime

    Use the pause and stop buttons in the top right corner whenever you need a break or you're done.

Tip: The AI motivation audio plays during your session. Keep your volume up so you can hear your motivator keeping you going.
Step 07

After Your Workout

When you stop your session, iSpeak celebrates your effort and reminds you how far you've come.

Post-Workout Summary

  • 1

    View Your Session Summary

    The end screen shows your total workout time for the session you just completed.

  • 2

    Check Your Streak

    Your current day streak is displayed — completing back-to-back days grows your streak and keeps momentum going.

  • 3

    See Your Session Details

    Your voice and intensity mode for that session are shown — Coach Mike on Standard for example — so you can track what worked.

  • 4

    One Step Closer To Goal

    iSpeak reminds you that every session brings you closer to what you set out to achieve. Tap Back To Home to return.

Step 08

Tracking Your Progress

The Progress tab is your personal improvement dashboard — see what's working and what's driving your results.

Progress Screen

  • 1

    Tap Progress in the Nav Bar

    Hit the Progress icon in the bottom navigation bar from any screen to open your dashboard.

  • 2

    Day Streak

    Your current streak and your longest streak ever are displayed at the top — a motivating reminder to keep the chain alive.

  • 3

    Total Workouts & Most Used Voice

    See exactly how many total sessions you've completed and which voice keeps you coming back — so you know what motivates you most.

  • 4

    Preferred Intensity Mode

    Your most-used mode (Light, Standard, or Intense) shows you the style of workout you naturally gravitate toward.

  • 5

    Workout History

    Scroll down to see a full log of past sessions by date, duration, and the voice used — your complete track record.

Step 09

Managing Your Profile

Add new goals, update your reward, and keep your iSpeak experience fresh as your fitness journey evolves.

Profile Screen

  • 1

    Tap Profile in the Nav Bar

    Hit the Profile icon on the far right of the bottom navigation bar.

  • 2

    View Your Active Goals

    Your current goals are listed with their status — Active or Complete — and their target dates.

  • 3

    Add Another Goal

    Tap + Add Another Goal to set a new goal alongside your existing one. You can work toward multiple goals at once.

  • 4

    Manage Your Rewards

    Scroll down to see your upcoming rewards and tap + Add New Reward to give yourself something to look forward to.

Tip: Keep adding goals and rewards as you complete them. The more specific your goal, the more personalized your AI motivation becomes.
